The major problem: Bonus Challenge answer key is wrong

The Student Workbook asks students to sequence these seven events. The Teacher Guide now includes a Bonus Challenge Answer Key, which is good — but the order is not correct.

The Teacher Guide says:

Deta and Heidi climb from Maienfeld, Barbara joins
Heidi wanders away with Peter and the goats
Heidi takes off her clothes
Deta gives Peter the coin
Deta says goodbye to Barbara near the hut
Heidi arrives at Grandfather’s hut
Deta tells Grandfather it is his turn

That is wrong because Deta says goodbye to Barbara before Heidi takes off her clothes and before Deta gives Peter the coin.

Correct Bonus Challenge order

Based on the chapter, the correct story order is:

Correct Order	Workbook Event
1	Deta and Heidi climb the mountain path from Maienfeld, stopping when Barbara comes out of her cottage to join them.
2	Heidi wanders away from Deta and Barbara and begins climbing the mountainside with Peter and his goats.
3	Deta said goodbye to Barbara near this hut, and Barbara continued her journey up the mountain.
4	Heidi takes off her shoes, socks, shawl, and extra dresses and leaves them in a pile on the hillside.
5	Deta gives Peter a five-penny coin to run back down and fetch Heidi’s clothes. “Peter didn’t often get such a treasure.”
6	Heidi, Peter, and Deta arrive at Grandfather’s hut, and Heidi calls out, “Good evening, Grandfather!”
7	Deta tells Grandfather, “I’ve taken care of Heidi for the last four years. Now it’s your turn to look after her,” and it was an adventure for Heidi, and she was eager to explore every corner.
