Best reusable pattern for your app

For your generator, I would teach Claude/code this distinction:

Think About the Story
Ask: “Can the answer be found directly in one place?”
Good wording: Who, what, when, where, how many, what did

Reading Between the Lines
Ask: “Does the student need to use clues?”
Good wording: Why might, what does this suggest, what clues show, why does the character

Dig Deeper
Ask: “Does the student need to explain a bigger idea?”
Good wording: How does, compare, what lesson, what theme, why is this important, what does this reveal
